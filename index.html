yt-uploader

A personal, single-user command-line client for the YouTube Data API.

What this application is

yt-uploader is a private tool that the owner of this repository runs on his own computer to manage YouTube channels that he owns and operates. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no website where third parties can log in, and no data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

What it does

  • uploads videos and starts live streams on channels owned by the account holder;
  • reads and updates those channels' own metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, categories, thumbnails, playlists);
  • reads the owner's YouTube Analytics reports to see how his own videos perform.

It acts only on channels that the signed-in Google Account already controls. It cannot access, read, or modify any other person's channel, videos, or data.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of the Google Account that grants access. The channels managed through it are CodeGreenCheck, LifeMusic, HiddenHistory and ForgottenLands, all operated by the same owner.

Data

OAuth credentials are stored as files on the owner's own machine. Nothing is sent to any third-party server. See the privacy policy for details.
